COMPANY EMPLOYEE POLICY

1. WORKING HOURS
   Employees are expected to work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees are entitled to a one-hour lunch break.

2. SICK LEAVE
   Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave may be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or medical conditions.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE
   Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Employees should request annual leave at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

4. WORK FROM HOME
   Employees may work from home for up to two days per week with approval from their manager. Employees must remain available during normal working hours.

5. MEDICAL INSURANCE
   The company provides medical insurance coverage for all full-time employees. Employees should contact the Human Resources department for information about insurance coverage.

6. EMPLOYEE CONDUCT
   Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior and treat colleagues with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated.

7. NOTICE PERIOD
   Employees who wish to resign must provide a 30-day notice period unless otherwise specified in their employment contract.
